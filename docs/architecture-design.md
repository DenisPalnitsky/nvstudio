# What services/architectural-design page is (and what it is NOT)

### This page is:

* A **sales + SEO landing page**
* For people searching:

  * *Residential architect GTA*
  * *Custom home architect Toronto*
  * *Residential architecture studio Ontario*
* For **both**:

  * Homeowners
  * Developers / builders

### This page is NOT:

* A technical spec
* A marketing fluff page
* A place to list every small service separately

Think of it as:

> “If Google could rank only ONE page for what you do — this would be it.”

---

# Core idea of the page

**Architectural Design = the umbrella service**

Everything else (permits, zoning, feasibility, coordination, renders) is positioned as:

> *part of a complete architectural design process*

This matches:

* How clients think
* How Google understands services
* How you avoid fragmenting SEO

---

# What MUST be on this page (section by section)

## 1️⃣ Hero section (above the fold)

**Purpose:** instantly answer *“Are you the right architect for my project?”*

**Contains:**

* Clear H1:

  > **Residential Architectural Design in the Greater Toronto Area**
* 1–2 sentence positioning:

  > Custom homes, renovations, and additions for homeowners and developers across the GTA and Ontario.
* CTA:

  > **Book a Consultation**

No jargon. No philosophy yet.

---

## 2️⃣ Who this service is for

**Purpose:** qualify the visitor.

Example:

* Homeowners planning a custom home
* Clients renovating or adding to an existing house
* Developers/builders needing permit-ready drawings

This helps **conversion** and reduces bad leads.

---

## 3️⃣ What “Architectural Design” includes (CRITICAL)

This is where most studios fail.

### Structure it like this:

**Architectural Design includes:**

* Concept & schematic design
* Zoning review & feasibility analysis
* Permit drawings & coordination with the city
* Design development & technical drawings
* Coordination with consultants (structural, mechanical, etc.)
* Design intent support during construction

👉 This section **absorbs**:

* Zoning
* Permits
* Feasibility
* Renders

No separate SEO pages needed.

---

## 4️⃣ Types of projects you do

**This is an SEO gold section.**

Example:

* Custom single-family homes
* Residential renovations
* Additions and extensions
* Small-scale residential developments

Each bullet reinforces relevance for **residential architecture searches**.

---

## 5️⃣ Your design approach (light, not fluffy)

**Purpose:** differentiate without over-texting.

Structure:

* How you start projects
* How you balance design + regulations
* How you work with builders and developers

Keep it concrete, not philosophical.

3. Design development
4. Permit drawings & approvals
5. Construction coordination

This reassures first-time clients.

## 7️⃣ Project examples (embedded)

**Very important.**

Show:

* 2–4 relevant residential projects
* With:

  * Image
  * Short description
  * Link to full project page

This passes SEO authority **to project pages** and boosts trust.

---

## 8️⃣ Why work with you (trust signals)

Short, factual:

* Residential focus
* Experience with GTA zoning & permits
* Clear communication with builders
* Hands-on studio approach

Avoid awards if you don’t have them — honesty > fluff.

## 9️⃣ Call to action (again)

End strong:

> **Planning a residential project in the GTA? Book a consultation.**
