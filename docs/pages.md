# Website Pages – Short Implementation Guide

---

## 1. Home ⭐

**Purpose**

* Instantly communicate *what you do*, *for whom*, and *where*
* Act as the main SEO authority page

**Content recommendations**

* Clear H1: *Residential Architecture Studio in the GTA*
* 1–2 sentence positioning statement
* Links to:

  * Architectural Design
  * Interior Design
  * Portfolio
* 2–3 featured projects
* Short “Why work with us” section
* Strong CTA: *Book a consultation*

**Implementation notes**

* Text is critical — not just images
* Must load fast (image optimization)
* Internal links are essential

---

## 2. Services – Overview

**Purpose**

* Orient users
* Route them to core services

**Content recommendations**

* Short intro paragraph
* Cards for:

  * Architectural Design
  * Interior Design
  * Planning & Permits
* Each card links to a dedicated page

**Implementation notes**

* Keep minimal
* Not an SEO-heavy page

---

## 3. Architectural Design (Residential) ⭐

**Purpose**

* Primary SEO landing page
* Convert residential clients

**Content recommendations**

* H1 with location (GTA / Ontario)
* Who this service is for
* What architectural design includes (umbrella list)
* Project types
* Short process overview
* Embedded project examples
* CTA

**Implementation notes**

* 900–1,400 words
* Must mention:

  * GTA
  * Ontario
  * Permits & zoning (as part of service)
* This page should internally link to:

  * Portfolio
  * Process
  * Contact

---

## 4. Interior Design ⭐

**Purpose**

* Capture separate interior design demand
* Support upselling

**Content recommendations**

* Residential interior focus
* Scope of interior services
* Relationship to architectural design
* 2–3 interior-focused projects

**Implementation notes**

* Can reuse structure of Architectural Design
* Keep wording non-generic

---

## 5. Planning & Building Permits

**Purpose**

* Educate first-time clients
* Capture small / technical projects

**Content recommendations**

* Explain zoning & feasibility
* Permit drawings & city coordination
* When this service is relevant
* Clarify it’s part of architectural design

**Implementation notes**

* Lower SEO priority
* Important for trust & clarity
* Avoid overly technical language

---

## 6. Process

**Purpose**

* Reduce friction
* Build confidence

**Content recommendations**

* Clear step-by-step process
* What client provides at each stage
* Where decisions happen

**Implementation notes**

* Diagrams work well
* Link from service pages
* Not SEO-critical

---

## 7. Portfolio – Index ⭐

**Purpose**

* Showcase work
* Funnel users to project pages

**Content recommendations**

* Grid of projects
* Filters:

  * Year
  * Project type (optional)

**Implementation notes**

* Each project must link to its own page
* Avoid infinite scroll without pagination

---

## 8. Individual Project Pages ⭐⭐⭐

**Purpose**

* Strongest SEO + trust asset
* Prove real experience

**Content recommendations**

* Project title + year
* City (not exact address)
* Client goals
* Design challenges
* Plans / drawings / renders
* Short narrative

**Implementation notes**

* One URL per project
* Text is mandatory (not image-only)
* Use consistent structure for all projects

---

## 9. About ⭐

**Purpose**

* Build personal trust
* Communicate philosophy & values

**Content recommendations**

* Studio story
* Personal background
* Design philosophy
* How you work with clients/builders

**Implementation notes**

* Authentic tone
* Include photo(s)
* Avoid buzzwords

---

## 10. Insights / Articles (optional)

**Purpose**

* Long-term SEO growth
* Educate clients

**Content recommendations**

* Practical topics only
* Regulations, costs, process

**Implementation notes**

* Don’t launch empty
* Only publish when content is useful

---

## 11. Contact / Book Consultation ⭐

**Purpose**

* Convert interest into action

**Content recommendations**

* Simple form
* Clear CTA
* What happens after submission

**Implementation notes**

* Link from every page
* No friction (minimal fields)

---

# Global Implementation Rules (IMPORTANT)

### Navigation

```
Home
Services
Process
Portfolio
About
Contact
```

### SEO basics

* One H1 per page
* Clean URLs
* Internal linking everywhere
* Images always with alt text

### Visual balance

* Strong visuals
* But never image-only pages
* Avoid visual clutter
